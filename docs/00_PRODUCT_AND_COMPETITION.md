# MOTERVA — Product charter and competition contract

**Status: proposed, not implemented.** Repo name is `Morteva`; product display name is MOTERVA. Do not silently rename the repository.

## User, job, business
First buyer: commercial inspection-fleet operator's operations/finance leader. The job is to fund authorized mission needs, safeguard reserves, coordinate cross-currency vendors, and verify paid-versus-delivered status. An organization configures financial authority; physical systems never receive unrestricted banking credentials.

Product promise: **mission-scoped financial autonomy, governed by evidence and reconciled against actual provider results.** Adapt to inspection drones, warehouse robots or underwater survey assets through typed operating-event adapters. Do not build separate finance logic per vehicle. Long-term autonomy is a thesis to validate with customer interviews, not a claim of traction.

## Primary customer flows
1. Configure an organization, mission, permitted suppliers/rails, spending cap, reserve floor, approval threshold, expiry and objective.
2. Read provider account balances; display timestamp, environment and uncommitted spendable funds separately from reservations.
3. Ingest a simulated or validated physical event with provenance/freshness; compare feasible financial options.
4. Within authority, decide to acquire more information via a mandated Metal testnet payment only if financially justified; record delivery and on-chain evidence separately.
5. Re-evaluate the plan, enforce exact economic terms and obtain a human approval if required.
6. Execute through Airwallex sandbox, reconcile ambiguous results, and independently record operational outcome.
7. Present a replayable case with reasons, denials, funding changes, real receipts and explicit simulated results.

## Non-goals for hackathon
No actual drone/vehicle control; no safety-critical automation; no real funds, loans or financial advice; no fake fiat-to-Metal bridge; no fabricated client deployments; no Waymo affiliation. No generalized ERP, no self-hosted large model training.

## Award strategy (not a prediction of winning)
- Founder’s Choice ($30k): buyer, economic value, novel end-to-end experience, credible expansion.
- Judges Choice ($20k): verified finance integration, robust agent decisions, controls, reproducible test suite.
- Metal ($15k): independent signed mandate-controlled Metal testnet transaction with changed downstream financial decision, plus Airwallex sandbox workflow.
- Visa ($15k): conditional only after sponsor confirms eligible, usable Visa tooling. A locally written imitation is insufficient.
The existence of four cash awards does not establish multi-award stacking; ask organizers.
Sponsor logos and cloud credits are not substitutes for genuine product value.

## Truth standard
Document each claim as VERIFIED_PROVIDER, VERIFIED_CODE, SIMULATED_OPERATION, PROPOSED, or BLOCKED. Provider creation is not settlement. Physical simulation is not recovery of a real machine.
